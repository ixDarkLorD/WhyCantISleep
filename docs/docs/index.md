# Documentation

Why Can't I Sleep? turns "You may not rest now" into a night you can do something about. These pages explain every
part of it: what players see and do, what server owners can set, what data packs can change, and what other mods can
build on.

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   **Playing**

    ---

    Start with [Getting started](playing/getting-started.md), then read about
    [why you can't sleep](playing/why-cant-i-sleep.md), [hiding](playing/hiding.md), [your nights](playing/nights.md)
    and [sleeping together](playing/together.md) on a server.

-   **Server owners**

    ---

    Every option in [Configuration](server/configuration.md), with its default, and the mod's
    [commands](server/commands.md).

-   **Data packs**

    ---

    Change what blocks sleep and what hiding ignores with [tags](datapacks/index.md), and add your own
    [sleep modifiers](datapacks/sleep-modifiers.md), [sleep effects](datapacks/sleep-effects.md) and
    [hiding spots](datapacks/hiding-spots.md), no code needed.

-   **Mod developers**

    ---

    Read a player's sleep, start and stop hiding, add hiding spots and listen to events with the
    [API](developers/index.md).

</div>

## What's in the mod

| Part | In short | Page |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monster highlights | The exact monsters that blocked your sleep glow, colored by how dangerous they are. | [Why can't I sleep?](playing/why-cant-i-sleep.md) |
| Arrows and rings | Point to blockers off screen or behind walls. | [Why can't I sleep?](playing/why-cant-i-sleep.md) |
| Sleep cards | Every failed sleep explained, with a hint. | [Why can't I sleep?](playing/why-cant-i-sleep.md) |
| Hiding | Under beds and tables, in chests and closets; noise, the heartbeat minigame, searches. | [Hiding](playing/hiding.md) |
| Sleep quality | Each night scored from the room, the light, monsters, music and more. | [Nights](playing/nights.md) |
| Sleep effects | Dreams, good and bad, on waking in the morning. | [Nights](playing/nights.md) |
| Night terrors | Sleep paralysis for those who stay up too long. | [Nights](playing/nights.md) |
| Sleep debt moon | Fills up for each day you go without sleep. | [Nights](playing/nights.md) |
| Bedtime panel | Who's still awake on a server, nudges and votes. | [Sleeping together](playing/together.md) |
| Insomniac's Handbook | The in-game guide book, given on your first join. | [Getting started](playing/getting-started.md) |

!!! note "Versions"
    Everything here is the same on every version the mod runs on: 26.1 to 26.3 (Fabric, NeoForge), 1.21.1 (Fabric,
    NeoForge, Forge) and 1.20.1 (Fabric, Forge). Where a detail differs, the page says so; pick your version in the
    header to see only what applies to you.
