# Getting started

This page gets the mod installed and walks you through your first night with it.

## Install

1. Install the mod loader for your version: Fabric, NeoForge or Forge (see the table below).
2. Download Why Can't I Sleep? from [Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/mod/why-cant-i-sleep) or
   [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/why-cant-i-sleep), and put it in your `mods` folder.
3. Add what it needs:
    - [CoolCatLib](https://modrinth.com/mod/coolcatlib), the library it is built on;
    - [Folios](https://modrinth.com/mod/folios), which draws its guide book;
    - [Fabric API](https://modrinth.com/mod/fabric-api), on Fabric only.

| Minecraft | Loaders |
| --- | --- |
| 26.1 – 26.3 | Fabric, NeoForge |
| 1.21.1 | Fabric, NeoForge, Forge |
| 1.20.1 | Fabric, Forge |

### On a server

Install the mod **on the server and on every client**. Hiding, sleep quality, night terrors, the
sleep debt moon and the Bedtime panel need it on the server, because the server decides them.

!!! tip "Optional: Simple Voice Chat"
    With [Simple Voice Chat](https://modrinth.com/plugin/simple-voice-chat) installed, your voice counts while you
    hide, and others hear you muffled through your hiding place. See [Hiding](hiding.md#voice-chat).

## Your first night

1. **Open the Insomniac's Handbook.** It is given to you the first time you join a world. Lost it? Craft another from
   a book and any bed. It covers everything on these pages, in game.
2. **Try to sleep with a monster nearby.** Instead of the game's line, a card tells you what's wrong and what to do,
   the monsters responsible glow, and arrows point to the ones you can't see.
   [Why can't I sleep?](why-cant-i-sleep.md)
3. **Hide.** Sneak and use a bed with empty hands to crawl under it. Keep still until whatever was after you gives up.
   [Hiding](hiding.md)
4. **Sleep, and see how well you slept.** In the morning a card scores your night and says what mattered most.
   [Nights](nights.md)
5. **Check on yourself.** In your inventory, the little moon by your player opens how you've been sleeping. Stay up a
   whole day and a moon appears on your screen, filling up as the days pass.

## Make it yours

Everything can be turned off or adjusted, from the glow's color to night terrors. Players change their own client
settings; server owners decide what everyone on the server gets. See [Configuration](../server/configuration.md).
