# Insomniac's Handbook

The **Insomniac's Handbook**, by The Sleepless Knight, is the mod's guide book. You're given one the first time you
join a world (the server can turn this off). If you lose it, craft another from **a book and any bed**. It has its own
tab in the creative menu.

It covers sleeping, hiding, sleep quality and dreams, with live lists of the sleep effects, the factors, the bedtime
songs and the soothing and unsettling things, and two quests: the **Bedtime Checklist** and **Hide and Seek**. The
book needs Folios (see [Compatibility](../docs/compatibility.md)).

## Quests

Two pages of the book carry a quest, a checklist that ticks itself off as you play. Finish one for a small reward.

### Bedtime Checklist

On the **How Well You Slept** page. In any order:

- Find a bed
- Find something soothing
- Get a jukebox for music
- Read How Well You Slept
- Sleep the night through
- and a last step that only shows once you've done it: wake [well rested](nights/sleep-quality.md#well-rested).

**Reward**: a flower pot, and one soothing plant picked at random (a lily of the valley, a cornflower, an azure bluet,
two moss carpets or an azalea).

### Hide and Seek

On the **How to Hide** page. In order:

1. Read How to Hide
2. Read Hiding Spots
3. Hide until what hunts you gives up ([shake them off](hiding/index.md#shaking-them-off)).

**Reward**: an oak door, to build a [closet](hiding/spots.md#closets) with.
