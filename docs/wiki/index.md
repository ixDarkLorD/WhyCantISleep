# Wiki

what makes a night good or bad. For installing it, its settings, data packs and the API, see the [Docs](../docs/index.md).

## At a glance

| Part | In short | Page |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sleep cards | Every failed sleep explained, with a hint. | [Why can't I sleep?](sleeping/why-cant-i-sleep.md) |
| Monster highlights | The exact monsters that blocked your sleep glow, colored by how dangerous they are. | [Monsters in the way](sleeping/monsters.md) |

| Noise | Moving, and with Simple Voice Chat talking, gives you away. | [Noise and searches](hiding/noise.md) |

!!! note "Versions"
    The game plays the same on every version the mod runs on: 26.1 to 26.3, 1.21.1 and 1.20.1. Where a detail differs,
