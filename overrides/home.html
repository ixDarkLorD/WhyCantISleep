No more guessing

A card for every reason

The game's one vague line is gone. In its place a card says what went wrong, and how to put it right, while the monsters that blocked you glow and arrows lead you to them.

  • Monsters nearby, the wrong time, too far, an obstructed or occupied bed
  • A warning before a bed that explodes in the Nether or the End
  • "The room is safe" once you've dealt with every one of them
  • A sleep debt moon that fills up for each day you stay awake

Sneak and use, with empty hands

Four places to hide

Nothing can see you while you hide, bosses aside. The world comes to you muffled through your hiding place, and so does your voice to others, with Simple Voice Chat.
