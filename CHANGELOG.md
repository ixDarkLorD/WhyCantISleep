# Changelog
This file is for listing all the changes to this project

## v26.1.2-1 Release | Unreleased
### ✨ New Features
- Project set up for Fabric and NeoForge (Minecraft 26.1 to 26.1.2), built on CoolCatLib: Core and CoolCatLib: Canvas.
- **Monster highlights**: when monsters keep you from sleeping, exactly the ones that blocked it (the game's own check range) glow for a few seconds, colored by the kind of threat or in one color. Only you see it; a server can also show it to players without the mod through the game's glowing effect.
- **Off-screen arrows**: arrows around the crosshair point to the blocking monsters you can't see, those outside your view and those behind blocks, in the same colors as their highlights and in three sizes. A solid arrow is a monster outside your view, a hollow one a monster behind blocks; nearer monsters get larger arrows, and arrows that would overlap fan apart.
- **Sleep HUD**: sleep messages no longer use the line above the hotbar. A card of the mod's own, drawn to match its status bars, shows an item for an icon, what happened and what to do about it; a blocked sleep reads "3 monsters nearby" over "Closest: Zombie, 6 blocks below". The sleep board is a card in the same style, with each awake player's face beside their name.
- **Visuals and sounds** (CoolCatLib: Canvas): a heartbeat and a red wash over the screen when monsters block a sleep, wisps rising off the blocking monsters in their highlight colors, whispers from where the nearest one is, and a rare "monster under the bed" scare: eyes at the bed's edge, darkness pouring out, a shriek and a jolt of the camera.
- **Sleep explanations**: every failed sleep says why and what to do: monsters nearby, the wrong time, too far from the bed, an obstructed or occupied bed, a night that can't be skipped, and how many more players must sleep.
- **Dimension warning**: the first use of a bed where beds explode (the Nether, the End) only warns; using it again within three seconds goes through.
- **Sleep board** (needs the mod on the server): while you're in bed, a panel lists who is still awake and why (awake, mining, fighting, AFK, in another dimension) with the progress toward the `playersSleepingPercentage` rule.
- **Nudges**: click an awake player on the board to send them a bell and a message, with a cooldown. Players without the mod get them too.
- **Mood and lucidity**: two bars, shown above your inventory. Your mood follows how you live: sleeping now and then, not going hungry and seeing the open sky keep it steady; a varied diet, feeding animals, company and a good night's sleep lift it; getting hurt, dying and a bad conscience weigh on it for a while. Your lucidity recovers while your mood is good and wears away while it is low, and takes a blow at once from killing a villager, a pet, a young animal or a golem, or from attacking a player who isn't fighting back. Each holds the other back: a clouded mind can't be wholly at ease, and a mind doesn't mend in misery; a line on each bar marks how far the other lets it fill, and the part beyond it is striped. A medal under your crosshair and a word of warning tell you when either isn't holding.
- **The thing under the bed**: let your lucidity fall and it comes. First there are eyes under dark beds and whispers; then it comes out, a figure only you see that is gone when you look at it; ignore it long enough and it becomes real and hunts you. Sleeping in the light eases the dread, and killing the creature ends it. When it comes it only watches at first: your world goes dark but for one light, which falls from wherever it stands. Look at it and it hunts you; never look and it leaves, but each time it is ignored it comes back less patient, calling to you and drawing your view round towards it unless you turn away. Hunted, you can lose it by getting well clear and staying clear. With it near, color drains from the world and darkness closes in from the edges of your view; as it comes for you a heartbeat quickens, each beat flushing the edges red. The "Scary content" option turns all of it off for you, and "Bed creature" turns it off for a whole server.
- **Kinds of Lurker**: what comes for you is one of four. The Stalker only has patience and teeth. The Flicker is half there, and steps behind you whenever you look away from it. The Brute is huge and slow, and its roar slows you, blinds you and shakes your lucidity. The Leech is small and quicker than you can run; its bite does little harm but leaves you starving, weak and miserable, and feeds it.
- **The Lucid Deep** (first part): the dream the Lurkers come from. Fall asleep holding a Dream Lens with a clear mind and you slip under: the view rises away from you asleep in bed while smoke and black tentacles creep in from the edges of the screen, and with no loading screen you are falling face first out of a dark, stormy sky of churning cloud, floating islands, ash and red lightning, seen from above as you flail, onto a small island, where you pick yourself up and look up, terrified, at what is in the sky. High in the north a glowing rift is torn in the sky, and through it you can see yourself asleep in your bed, as it is right now; hold the lens up to it to wake with whatever you found. Die there, fall off the world or run out of lucidity and you wake too, but what you found fades. Every so often, something in the clouds opens its eyes. Its creatures, bosses and treasures are still to come.
- The mod is now needed on both the client and the server.
- Client and server configs, with client options locked while the server turns their feature off.
